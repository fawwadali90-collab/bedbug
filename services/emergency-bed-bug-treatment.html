Rapid Response

When You Need Help Now, Not Next Week

Finding bed bugs is one of the most stressful discoveries a homeowner or hotel manager can make. Your skin crawls, sleep feels impossible, and every shadow looks suspicious. Take a breath — this is fixable, and it is fixable fast. We offer same-day inspections across Naples because we know that waiting a week for an appointment is not an option when you have found bed bugs in your bed. Hotels and rental managers feel a different kind of pressure — a guest complaint with a morning checkout deadline. We treat those calls with the same urgency, and we know how to handle the situation without alarming other guests.

What you do tonight matters. Do not move to the couch — that just spreads bed bugs to another room. Do not throw the mattress out yet — we need to inspect it, and dragging it through the house scatters them further. Instead: strip the bed into sealed bags, keep your belongings contained, and call us. If you already used a store-bought spray, don't worry — just tell us so we know what we're working around. And save any bug you found in a sealed bag or jar; a confirmed sample speeds everything up. We will tell you exactly how to get through the night, then confirm the problem and start treatment as quickly as possible.

  • Same-day inspections across Naples and nearby areas
  • Clear tonight-action plan so you don't spread them
  • Rapid treatment scheduling — heat, chemical, or K9 first

Do These Three Things Tonight

One: stay in the bed — moving rooms spreads them. Two: bag your bedding and clothes in sealed bags. Three: call us before you throw anything away. Premature disposal almost always makes the problem worse, not better.
