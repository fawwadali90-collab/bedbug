Prevention Beats Treatment Every Time

Naples is a crossroads of travel. Tourists cycle through the resorts on Vanderbilt Beach, seasonal residents fly home from around the world, and every suitcase is a potential delivery vehicle for bed bugs. You cannot control where bed bugs come from — but you can control whether they get a foothold. A prevention plan turns your property from an easy target into a hard one.

Our plans are built around how you actually live here. Vacation rental hosts get between-booking inspection protocols and mattress protection for every bed they rent out. Seasonal residents get a pre-arrival sweep and a travel checklist for the trip down. Property managers get scheduled K9 sweeps across their portfolio. Whatever your situation, the plan is the same idea: catch bed bugs early, when they are easiest to stop. The math is simple — a scheduled inspection costs far less than a full treatment, and it protects something treatment can't: your peace of mind, your reviews, and your guests' trust.

  • Scheduled inspections and K9 sweeps for rentals
  • Travel and luggage checklists for seasonal residents
  • Host protocols for Airbnb and vacation rentals

Simple Process

How Our Prevention Plans Work

A simple four-step setup, then quiet protection in the background.

Risk Assessment

We look at your property, your travel habits, and your exposure — rental turnover, frequent travel, shared condo walls — and identify where bed bugs are most likely to enter.

Your Custom Protocol

You get a written plan: inspection frequency, what to check and when, travel and luggage habits, and exactly what to do if you spot something suspicious.

Install Monitoring

We place discreet monitoring devices in high-risk spots and fit mattress encasements where they make sense, so early signs don't go unnoticed.

Stay Ahead

Scheduled re-inspections or K9 sweeps keep the plan working year after year — especially valuable for rentals and homes that sit empty between seasons.

Common Questions

Prevention Plan Questions, Answered

A written protocol tailored to your property: scheduled inspections, monitoring device placement, mattress encasement recommendations, travel and luggage habits, and clear instructions for what to do if you spot a warning sign.

Inspect between bookings, encase every mattress and box spring, and schedule periodic professional sweeps — especially before peak season. A plan gives your cleaners a simple checklist and gives you documentation that protects your listing if a guest ever complains.

Pull back the sheets and check the mattress seams, box spring edges, and headboard with your phone flashlight. Look for rust-colored spots, shed skins, or live bugs. Keep luggage on the rack — never on the bed — and inspect your suitcase before bringing it home.

It can be, with caution. Inspect seams, joints, and undersides carefully before it enters your home, and consider having us check larger pieces. Upholstered furniture and mattresses carry the most risk; hard furniture is generally safer.

We recommend a quick check between every booking by your cleaning team, plus a professional inspection or K9 sweep on a regular schedule — more often during peak season when turnover is highest. High-turnover properties benefit from quarterly professional sweeps.

Empty homes are not safe homes — bed bugs can survive months without feeding. We recommend a pre-arrival inspection before you return for the season, encasements on all beds, and monitoring devices that quietly watch the property while you are away.
