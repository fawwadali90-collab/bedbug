Whole-Home Solutions

A Treatment Plan Built Around Your Home

Bed bugs rarely stay in one bedroom. They travel along baseboards, hitch rides on laundry, and set up satellite hiding spots in living rooms, kids' rooms, and home offices. That is why treating a single room almost always fails — and why our residential service starts with a full whole-home inspection. We map every harborage, from the master suite to the guest room that only gets used in season, then build a plan that addresses the entire infestation at once.

Naples homes come with their own realities. Condos in Pelican Bay and high-rises in Park Shore share walls where a neighbor's problem can become yours, and most HOAs want a heads-up before work begins — we coordinate directly with property managers and schedule discreetly. Families get a clear prep checklist, honest guidance about kids and pets during treatment, and straight answers at every step, because nobody should have to navigate this alone. Seasonal residents face another wrinkle: guest rooms that sit empty for months can still harbor bed bugs, since they survive long stretches without feeding. We always include those quiet rooms in the inspection, and we recommend encasements on every bed in the house as part of the follow-up.

  • Whole-home inspection — not just the bedroom
  • HOA and property-manager coordination included
  • Clear prep checklist your whole family can follow

We Treat the Home, Not Just the Room

Spot treatments chase bed bugs from room to room. A whole-home plan — inspection, targeted treatment, and mattress protection together — is what actually ends an infestation. From Old Naples cottages to new builds in Ave Maria, the principle holds: find them all, treat them all, protect the beds. We will tell you honestly which approach your situation needs.
