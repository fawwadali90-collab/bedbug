Why a Professional Bed Bug Inspection Comes First

Every year, Naples welcomes a fresh wave of visitors and seasonal residents back into condos that sat empty for months. A guest's suitcase, a vacation-rental turnover on Marco Island, or a stay in a busy resort along the tourism corridor can carry bed bugs home quietly — and by the time bites show up, the problem already has a head start. The trouble is that the early signs mimic other issues entirely: carpet beetle irritation, flea bites, even ordinary allergies. Treating on a hunch wastes money, and worse, a half-hearted DIY attempt can scatter a real infestation deeper into wall voids and neighboring units.

That's why everything we do starts with a professional bed bug inspection. We work methodically through every sleeping area — pulling back the sheets to examine mattress seams, piping, and tags; flipping box springs; checking headboards, bed frames, and nightstands joint by joint; then running a flashlight along baseboards, outlet covers, and wall voids. We also inspect luggage racks, upholstered furniture, and closets, looking for live bugs, shed skins, dark fecal spotting, and tiny pearl-white eggs. You get a clear written summary of what we found — and if treatment is warranted, an honest plan for exactly what it will take.
