Built for Business

Bed Bug Control Built for Naples Businesses

In a town built on tourism, a single bed bug complaint can travel further than a five-star review. From the resorts along Park Shore and Vanderbilt Beach to the boutique hotels near 5th Ave South and the vacation rentals scattered across Marco Island, hospitality businesses live and die by guest confidence. Bed bugs are not just a pest problem here — they are a business risk, and they need a response that protects revenue as well as rooms.

That is why our commercial service is built around discretion and continuity. We schedule during low-occupancy windows, treat rooms in rotation so you keep selling inventory, and arrive quietly — no marked trucks drawing a crowd in the parking lot. We coordinate with your housekeeping staff, train them to spot the earliest warning signs, and set up monitoring so a new introduction is caught before a guest ever notices. Multi-unit properties get special attention, too: when one apartment or condo unit is confirmed, adjacent units are inspected at the same time, because bed bugs travel through wall voids and shared plumbing chases. That whole-building view is what keeps a single incident from becoming a property-wide problem — and it is exactly what generalist pest companies skip.

  • Discreet arrival with unmarked scheduling options
  • Off-peak treatment windows that keep rooms rentable
  • Staff training and monitoring plans that prevent repeats
