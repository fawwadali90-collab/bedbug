Privacy Policy

Naples Bed Bug Treatment respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you call, email, or visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you contact us: your name, phone number, email address, and details about your property or situation so we can schedule an inspection and recommend the right bed bug treatment. If you email us photos, those images are stored with your correspondence.

When you visit our website, we also collect limited information automatically: your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view, and how long you spend on them. This helps us understand how visitors use the site and keep it running smoothly.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiries, schedule inspections and service visits, provide the bed bug treatments you request, and communicate with you about your appointment. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying website data to improve our pages, fix technical problems, and make the site easier to use.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to our bed bug services without telling you first. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to remember basic preferences and to measure how visitors use the site. We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools that record aggregate information such as pages visited and general location, without identifying you personally.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Most features of our site will still work, but some conveniences may be affected. Blocking analytics cookies will not prevent you from calling or emailing us.

Sharing Your Information

We share your information only when necessary. That includes service providers who help us operate the business, such as phone carriers, email hosts, or website analytics providers — they receive only what they need and are expected to protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law, such as in response to a court order or legal process.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

Your Rights and Choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate details, or ask us to delete your information where that is possible. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

You may also opt out of analytics tracking by disabling cookies in your browser, and you can unsubscribe from any non-essential emails by replying with your preference. Applicable privacy laws may give you additional rights depending on where you live.
