Quiet, Careful Bed Bug Care for Pelican Bay Residents

Pelican Bay pairs some of Naples' finest addresses with one of its trickiest bed bug patterns. High-rise towers and luxury villas sit behind gates, many of them part-time homes that close up for months while owners are away. Bed bugs do not mind the quiet — adults can survive a long stretch without feeding, so a unit that sat empty all summer can greet its owners with a live infestation in the fall. Add in guests, housekeepers, and luggage arriving for the season, and there are plenty of ways a new introduction begins.

Our service here is deliberately low-profile and careful. We coordinate with concierge desks, building security, and property managers so access and scheduling run smoothly. Treatment is planned around high-end interiors — monitored heat treatment, clear prep guidance, and mattress encasements that protect guest beds between seasons. And for seasonal residents, a pre-arrival inspection means you walk into a verified-clear home instead of an unwelcome surprise. Discretion is not a bonus here; it is the baseline.
