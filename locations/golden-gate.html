Stopping Fast-Spreading Bed Bugs in Golden Gate

Golden Gate is one of the most densely settled parts of Naples — apartment complexes, duplexes, and multi-family buildings where units share walls, plumbing, and hallways. That density is exactly what makes bed bugs so tricky here: they travel from unit to unit through wall voids and outlets, so treating one apartment while the neighbor goes unchecked just starts the cycle over. In Golden Gate, half-measures do not work; the whole picture has to be addressed at once.

Our multi-unit experience is the difference. We inspect the reported unit and the neighboring units together, coordinate with landlords and property managers on scheduling, and plan treatments so connected units are handled in the right order. Residents get a simple prep checklist and honest guidance — including when a mattress can be saved with an encasement instead of thrown out. Whether you are a tenant, a landlord, or a manager with a building to protect, you get the same careful, coordinated approach.
