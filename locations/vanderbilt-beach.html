Vanderbilt Beach is one of the busiest tourist corridors in North Naples — beachfront hotels, resort towers, and vacation rentals stacked along Gulf Shore Drive, each turning over guests week after week. Bed bugs thrive on exactly this pattern: new luggage in every room, every few days, year-round. For lodging operators here, a single guest complaint can mean a bad review, a lost booking, and a problem spreading to neighboring rooms while you decide what to do.

We are built for this environment. Our K9 teams sweep entire floors quickly to find exactly which rooms need treatment and which are clear — no closing down rooms that are fine.
