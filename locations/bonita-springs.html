Seasonal Homes and Beach Condos

For Homes That Sit Empty Half the Year

Bonita Springs has a rhythm all its own: condos lock up in spring, sit quiet through the summer heat, and come back to life when the snowbirds return. Here is the uncomfortable truth — bed bugs can survive months without feeding, waiting in mattress seams and baseboards for a host to come home. A unit that looked fine in April can have a full-blown problem by November, discovered the first night back.

We also treat the other side of Bonita life: year-round families in single-family neighborhoods, beach condos with steady rental turnover, and the luggage that comes home from every summer trip up north. Whether you need an inspection before you settle in for the season or a full treatment plan for a year-round home, one call gets a specialist who knows Bonita's seasonal pattern.

  • Seasonal inspections timed to your return — catch problems the first week back
  • Coordination with key holders and property watch services while you are away
  • Mattress encasements and prevention plans that guard empty-season units
