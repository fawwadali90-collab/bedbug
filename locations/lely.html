Lely is one of Naples' most settled communities — a blend of single-family homes, golf-course condos, and long-time residents mixed with seasonal owners who arrive each winter. Bed bugs find their way in through the usual channels: suitcases from summer trips, houseguests, visiting family, and the occasional secondhand find. In a community where neighbors know each other and privacy matters, most residents want the situation assessed honestly and resolved without fuss.

We schedule quietly, work around HOA and community procedures, and leave you with a prep checklist plus mattress encasements that keep beds protected. No scare tactics, no upsells — just a specialist doing the job right.
