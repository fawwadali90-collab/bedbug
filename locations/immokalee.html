Multi-Family and Workforce Housing

Built for How Immokalee Actually Lives

Immokalee's housing looks different from coastal Naples — more multi-family buildings, more shared walls, more people under one roof. That density is exactly what bed bugs exploit: they travel through wall voids, along pipes, and on shared laundry equipment from one unit to the next. Treating a single apartment while the neighboring unit goes unchecked is how infestations come back, which is why coordination matters as much as the treatment itself.

We work with residents, landlords, and property managers to handle the whole picture — inspecting adjacent units, scheduling around work hours, and keeping communication clear for everyone involved. Bed bugs are hitchhikers, not a reflection of cleanliness, and they affect every community in Southwest Florida. Our job is to confirm the problem, treat it thoroughly, and help keep it from returning.

  • Multi-unit coordination — adjacent units inspected when shared walls are involved
  • Flexible scheduling that works around agricultural work hours
  • Clear communication for residents, landlords, and managers alike
