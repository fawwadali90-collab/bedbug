Built for a Town That Is Always Moving in

Ave Maria runs on arrivals. Students roll in each semester with carloads of bedding and hand-me-down furniture, families settle into new planned neighborhoods, and rental turnover never really stops. Every move-in is a fresh chance for bed bugs to hitch a ride — tucked into a mattress, a box spring, or a thrifted couch. In a town built around a university, a small introduction can spread fast through shared housing and neighboring rentals.

We work with everyone in that picture: students in off-campus housing, families in new subdivisions, and landlords and property managers overseeing multiple doors.
