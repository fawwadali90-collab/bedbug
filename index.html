One Pest, One Focus

Naples Bed Bug Exterminators Who Do One Thing Well

Bed bugs do not care how clean your home is — they care that you are there. Naples is a natural crossroads for them: millions of visitors pass through the resorts along Park Shore and Vanderbilt Beach, Marco Island vacation rentals turn over guests weekly, and snowbirds return each season with luggage that may have picked up hitchhikers anywhere along the way. A single female bed bug tucked into a suitcase seam can start an infestation that spreads room to room before anyone notices a bite.

These pests are masters of hiding. They wedge into mattress seams, box spring edges, headboard cracks, baseboard gaps, and the joints of nightstands and dressers — anywhere within a few feet of where someone sleeps. Because they are nocturnal and their bites vary from person to person, many Naples homeowners live with a growing problem for weeks before they see one. That is why our first step is always a thorough inspection: we confirm the pest visually before recommending any treatment, so you never pay for a solution to a problem you do not have.

As bed bug specialists, we bring every proven approach to the table — whole-room heat treatment, targeted chemical applications, structural fumigation for severe cases, and trained K9 scent-detection teams for hard-to-find infestations. The recommendation follows the inspection, not the other way around, and every plan includes follow-up visits and prevention guidance so the problem stays solved.

  • Heat, chemical, fumigation, and K9 detection under one roof
  • Discreet service for homes, condos, hotels, and vacation rentals
  • Follow-up visits and prevention plans that keep them gone

Not Sure If It's Bed Bugs?

Itchy bites do not always mean bed bugs — mosquitoes, fleas, and skin reactions cause lookalike marks. The only way to know for sure is a visual inspection of the places they hide: mattress seams, headboards, box springs, and baseboards near the bed.
